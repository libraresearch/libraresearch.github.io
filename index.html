05 / ABOUT

Serious research.
No fluff.

Libra Research is an independent due diligence practice based in Oslo. We produce structured, analyst-grade dossiers on companies and individuals using open-source intelligence — fully auditable, no proprietary black boxes.

Our methodology is built on the same principles applied in regulated AML and financial crime investigations inside Norwegian financial institutions — systematic collection, rigorous verification, and full source documentation. What you receive is a report you can stand behind.

We work natively across Norwegian and Scandinavian registries — the same sources regulators and banks use — and extend internationally where the subject demands it, on mandates ranging from pre-acquisition screening to ongoing counterparty monitoring.
